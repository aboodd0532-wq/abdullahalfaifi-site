FAQ

Common questions

Can a company domain be connected to Microsoft 365 without moving the website?

Yes. The same domain can be used for Microsoft 365 email while the website remains on a separate hosting platform, using different DNS records.

Is MFA alone enough to protect user accounts?

MFA is essential, but stronger protection comes from combining it with Conditional Access, session controls, managed devices, and least-privilege policies.

What does Intune provide for businesses?

It provides centralized device, app, compliance, and company-data management across supported endpoints.

Are these services suitable for small and medium businesses?

Yes. Organizations can begin with essential controls and expand as user count, devices, licensing, and security needs grow.
